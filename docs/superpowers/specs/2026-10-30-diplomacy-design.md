# Diplomacy: first contact, settlement accords and trade

Date: 2026-10-30, America/Denver. **Status: complete proposal for owner review.** M11-D / DIP-AC-012 In Progress; M11-P/M11-I and functional criteria Not Started. The owner selected diplomacy and said, “Use your own judgement and put together the design.” This delegates the starting choices below; it does not record approval of this unread proposal or authorize product implementation.

## Intent and authority

Make meeting another civilization change the player's decisions: protect a developed system by giving up further settlement there, build a reliable relationship, then invest production in mutually beneficial commerce. Cooperation should compete with expansion and shipbuilding for attention and resources. Agreements must constrain the rival through the same commands that constrain humans.

Authorities: [charter](../../CHARTER.md), [engineering rules](../../../CLAUDE.md), [workflow](../../PROJECT_WORKFLOW.md), approved rival, colony, fleet, combat and ship-design specifications in this directory, and [M10 integration](../../../reports/SHIP_DESIGN_INTEGRATION_2026-10-30.md). Inspected main is 45c5cf0, clean before drafting, rules10/writer11/56 explicit schema/rules pairs. The existing integration evidence records 2030 passing simulation tests; no gameplay verification is claimed for this document.

The current combat system fights neutral sentries, not other civilizations. Existing rivals remain peaceful expansion competitors. There is no treasury/currency or freight network. A non-aggression pact would currently promise behavior already guaranteed. This proposal therefore substitutes an enforceable **settlement accord**, and uses existing colony production for trade. Empire war/declarations/peace, invasions, border movement exclusion, alliances, espionage, gifts, technology/map exchanges, bargaining bundles, leaders/portraits, new species, victory and FR-001 peaceful mode remain later scope. Passage, surveying, sentries, ameba, research, admirals and refits retain their existing rules.

Alternatives considered: diplomacy as flavor-only attitudes (too little strategic effect); immediately adding war/peace (requires a separate military-rival design); unrestricted barter (large economy and information surface). Recommend the two bounded agreements below. All numerical values are drafter choices, not measured balance.

## DIP-001 Contact and communication

Preserve observed encounters as the prerequisite. New-rules first contact establishes a **mutual communications channel**: when normal ContactUpdater would reveal B to A from A's legitimate observation, add reciprocal identity-only contact for B at that boundary if absent. This explicit radio introduction reveals species/culture and empire identity, never A's location, ship count or home. A retains its ordinary observed location; B's reciprocal introduction has no encounter-location field. Existing observed-colony reports remain viewer-local. Multiple simultaneous observations produce one channel and one first-contact notice per viewer/pair, ordered by EmpireId. Existing identity-only contact is retained permanently.

Contacts do not require continued co-location or a communication range. Communications permit offers without refreshing any surveys. Solo has an empty contacts list and an explanatory screen. No invisible empire appears in a list or target selector. The default one-rival setup, all four selectable species and existing mirroring remain. No new setup toggle or difficulty selector.

Opening contact shows original brief cultural text using existing descriptions and explains that civilizations currently compete peacefully for settlement. Do not invent species-specific diplomatic bonuses. Reuse existing identity presentation; no new art pipeline.

## DIP-002 Directed attitude and visible reasons

Keep one directed attitude from each empire toward each contacted counterpart. Initial score is 0. Each successfully completed boundary with at least one effective agreement increases that pair's cooperation streak by 1, capped at 10. No effective agreement resets the streak to 0. Score = 2 * streak - cancellation memory. Cancellation memory is 10 while a unilateral termination notice by the counterpart is less than 10 completed boundaries old, otherwise 0. Multiple cancellations refresh the memory date; they do not stack. Own cancellation does not lower one's own opinion. Score bounds are -10..20. No random rolls, military-strength term, hidden territorial pressure or global reputation.

Labels: Strained (<0), Neutral (0..9), Receptive (10..19), Cooperative (20). Both directed scores, their exact streak/memory contributions and remaining memory duration are public to the two parties. Never expose either party's relations with a third empire. Offer acceptance thresholds use the **recipient's** score toward the proposer. A decline or expiry has no penalty. No repeatable greeting/gift can farm attitude. Cultural flavor may differ; mechanical policy is the same for all four species in this milestone.

An agreement effective during boundary T->T+1 counts once even if it has a notice running; a newly accepted agreement in that boundary begins contributing at the next boundary. Cancellation memory begins when notice is resolved, so the outgoing boundary's AI decisions never see another empire's uncommitted action. Record reason timestamps rather than an unbounded event list.

## DIP-003 Offers, responses and atomic authority

One pending offer per unordered empire pair, across both agreement kinds; one effective agreement of each kind per pair. A pending offer cannot replace a live agreement of that kind, including a notice period. Offer contains pair-local monotonic ID, proposer/recipient, kind, created turn, expiry, exact public terms, proposer authorization and revision. No global entity ID or PRNG consumption. All offer/answer/withdraw/termination actions are ordinary issuer-tagged commands with ExpectedTurn and current visible pair revision; changed commands clear issuer readiness and invalidate its existing Ready confirmations normally. Ready empires cannot mutate orders without the normal readiness reset path. Foreign, forged, stale or uncontacted requests reject atomically with no resources/IDs consumed.

Submitting an offer creates a binding authorization, not an immediate treaty. It remains available through the next three resolution boundaries: created at planning turn T, valid for the resolutions starting T, T+1 and T+2; unanswered it expires during T+2->T+3. Human recipients see Accept/Decline; neither is automatic. No end-turn response is compulsory. Answers queue for resolution. Proposer may Withdraw before that resolution. A withdrawn offer defeats a queued answer, with a normal withdrawn result and no charge. An answered offer cannot be answered twice; identical command repetition is an accepted no-op. No-op preserves IDs, readiness and timestamps.

Human offers are visible only to the two parties. At a boundary, AI reads each pending offer in its permitted pre-resolution view and issues Accept/Decline through the same commands. AI does not read newly queued private human answers, future economy or other AI plans. Existing foreign human offers can therefore receive an AI answer on the submitting turn's resolution. AI-created offers are published for the next planning turn; no recursive same-boundary AI negotiation.

Resolve Withdraw first, then queued answers, then unanswered expiry, with stable pair/offer ordering. Valid answers resolve before expiry at the third boundary. Crossed new offers in multi-human play share the one-pending constraint: first accepted command wins and later commands return the visible existing-offer conflict; no automatic merged bargain. A command already accepted remains tentative until the full turn transaction succeeds. Failed resolution returns the original pre-Ready state, including all pending intentions, resource reservations, readiness, counters and reports. Distinguish expected offer failure from internal transaction failure: changed eligibility yields a normal “could not activate” result; overflow or inconsistent state aborts the entire turn.

Revoke Answer is legal for either a queued Accept or Decline before resolution. It clears the answer to unanswered and immediately releases any recipient earmark; the proposer earmark remains. The recipient may then Accept (selecting/revalidating a hub anew) or Decline while the original offer remains valid. Directly changing an answer requires Revoke first. Repeated identical answers/revocations are no-ops; an offer's original expiry never extends. Withdrawing/declining/expiring closes that offer permanently, so later answers cannot resurrect it.

An agreement accepted during T->T+1 becomes effective in the published state at T+1. It affects founding, trade earnings and attitude only for resolution T+1->T+2 and later. This prevents a late answer changing the meaning of this turn's paid founding or economic snapshot. Show the effective turn in every preview/report.

## DIP-004 Settlement accord

One accord covers **one named, already surveyed star system**. Both sides agree to found no additional colonies anywhere in that system while it is effective. Existing colonies, growth, production, travel and surveys continue. Nobody gets ownership or exclusive rights; unoccupied systems can remain contested elsewhere. This is a mutual freeze of settlement, not a non-aggression pact or military border.

The proposer must have a current legitimate observation of the system containing at least one owned or observed counterpart colony. The offer names the system and explicitly discloses the proposer's knowledge of it. The recipient must independently have surveyed it and, from its own current observation, see at least one colony belonging to either party there to Accept. Receiving an otherwise ineligible offer returns only “not ready for this agreement”; it does not reveal the recipient's map. Neither side receives current ownership facts by making a failed query. The proposer cannot inspect recipient eligibility before sending. Both parties can see the named terms once the offer exists, even if their own survey later becomes stale.

At activation, revalidate each party using its own permitted current observation and ensure both consents still exist. Do not perform hidden global ownership tests beyond ordinary command authority. The accord protects that system regardless of later unseen changes; the list never silently expands to new colonies. A third empire is unconstrained. No live colony count is disclosed by the accord.

Effective accords add a public founding restriction for their two signatories before ordinary founding validation. Human previews and AI candidate filtering use this same rule. Any queued founding intent submitted before activation is retained, marked Attention and prevented from resolving from the first effective boundary onward; no cargo, ship, population or production is lost, and the player may cancel/redirect normally. Never auto-consume a founder or repeatedly submit a known blocked AI order. Arrival and exploration remain legal. Expiry of the block restores ordinary validation, not guaranteed founding or ownership. Sentry/ameba restrictions remain independently applicable.

Recipient AI accepts a legal settlement accord at score >=0. The system must contain its own currently observed colony; it declines a proposal covering only the human's colony, because giving up expansion there would not protect an AI investment. This is an explicit conservative policy, not a hidden bargain score. Its own existing colony is always known; observed foreign facts remain ordinary knowledge. Show “would protect our settlement” or “we have no settlement to protect” as the public reason, intentionally disclosing only whether it has a colony in the offered system, never a count/location beyond that named system. This response disclosure is a deliberate diplomatic statement. AI may propose a system containing an owned colony and a currently observed human colony, ranked by StarId. Thus a genuine two-sided accord is most likely after a shared-system settlement encounter.

## DIP-005 Trade agreement and production accounting

Trade is a small bilateral production benefit from civilian exchange. It creates no cargo, routes, support transfer, research, military service, foreign port access or new money. Each signatory selects **one own hub colony** and authorizes a one-time setup payment of **500 internal production (5 displayed)** from its existing bank. Each subsequently earns **100 internal production (1 displayed)** per effective boundary at its own selected hub, after a **three-boundary warmup**. Neither side receives the other's production; trade output is explicitly newly generated exchange income.

Proposer selects its hub when submitting, recipient selects its own when accepting. Hub colony IDs, location, banks and forecasts are private to their owner; public offer terms state only costs, output, warmup and duration. The partner sees “partner committed a trade hub,” not affordability, readiness or selected colony. Eligible hub: owned and productive (positive ordinary NetProduction in the current own snapshot), no refit, no active assembly contribution, and enough unreserved production bank. Ordinary construction and unstarted queues may continue. Use current forecast, not population potential or future completion. Public rejection reasons never identify a hidden hub/bank; own UI provides exact private reasons.

Offering/accepting reserves the setup amount at that hub immediately through a persistent earmark. It is still part of the bank, never an extra account. Ordinary production completion/refit/assembly affordability treats earmarked amounts as unavailable; no other subsystem may spend them. A colony has at most one diplomacy earmark, so it cannot authorize two trade commitments against the same bank. The proposer may withdraw to free it, recipient may revoke its queued Accept before resolution, and expiry/decline/failed activation frees all earmarks. Earmarks do not prevent workforce/target changes or ordinary manual control. Preview shows total bank, reserved setup and spendable bank; any existing estimate/completion calculation must use spendable quantities. A setup cannot cancel a project implicitly.

Activation revalidates hubs against the turn-start own economic snapshot before ordinary completions. Affordability at this stage counts the offer's own earmark toward its payment: require bank >= that earmark and the exact matching authorization, rather than another 500 of unreserved funds. A bank of exactly 500 with its matching 500 earmark is sufficient. If either is no longer eligible, conclude the offer as failed, release both earmarks and charge neither side. Report only “trade could not activate”; each owner additionally receives its own failure reason. Otherwise deduct exactly 500 from each earmarked bank atomically and consume both earmarks; no discounts/refunds or same-boundary trade income. Ordinary economy then sees the reduced available bank. Insufficient funds after a supposedly valid earmark is a state invariant failure, not a partial payment.

Withdraw and Revoke release the applicable earmarks at command acceptance; Decline, unanswered expiry and failed activation release them at their boundary phase before ordinary production completions. Released funds can finish an ordinary project on that same boundary. Own forecasts include already committed withdrawals/answers and deterministic expiry; if another party's answer or eligibility is unresolved, show the ordinary estimate with funds still reserved plus the explicitly conditional estimate if released. Never predict a hidden partner decision as certain.

If accepted during T->T+1, the effective warmup boundaries are T+1->T+2, T+2->T+3 and T+3->T+4, with zero trade output; first +100 is T+4->T+5. Later output is added to the hub's **net production after ordinary workforce/support penalties**, before ordinary project/refit completion. It is not multiplied, discounted, added to gross workforce output, counted as research, or reduced by the same support penalty a second time. The same calculation feeds own forecasts and actual resolution, with a separate Trade +1 source. It can fund ordinary projects/refits once the agreement is live; setup hub restrictions apply at activation only. Output does not require continued positive workforce production, so workforce changes after setup cannot spy on a partner's output through suspension.

The hub cannot be switched or merged. If an owned hub ceases to exist or belong to its signatory, that signatory's output stops and the bilateral agreement automatically ends at the next boundary, before any new trade output. Both sides receive “trade ended: a partner's exchange link was lost”; the owner sees the actual hub reason. Such loss is not a betrayal or penalty; no setup refund. The initial milestone has no colony capture/destruction, but valid future/supplementary state must handle this without dangling references. No remote hub status queries are exposed.

## DIP-006 Duration, termination and deterministic AI

Both agreement types have a minimum of **10 effective boundaries**, then continue indefinitely. Either side can give termination notice once the minimum is completed. Notice lasts **two full further effective boundaries**; effects persist through both, then cease in the published state. Example: activation at T+1; ten effective boundaries complete at T+11; notice in planning T+11 resolves at T+12, effects continue for T+11->T+12 and T+12->T+13, ending at T+13. No revocation of a termination notice, instant breach, unpaid exit or hidden automatic renewal fee. The initiating party's cancellation lowers the counterpart's attitude from the notice-resolution publication, independently of the final end. If both give notice at the same boundary, both receive cancellation memory; use the earliest end and charge nothing. Only the ending agreement is removed. Trade hub loss is the sole early automatic ending in this slice.

The expansion AI preserves its prior policy, costs and knowledge boundaries. Diplomacy adds at most one response, one new offer and one termination per contacted counterpart per turn, with a single public deterministic policy ID. No additional RNG. It accepts trade at score >=10 and with a legal own hub; choose the lowest-ColonyId eligible hub. It does not promise funds it lacks. It accepts settlement as DIP-004. Accepted agreements constrain all later ordinary expansion commands.

AI new-offer priority: trade if legal score >=10, otherwise a qualifying settlement accord. Do not propose an already active kind or while any offer is pending. Choose smallest StarId for the accord and lowest-ColonyId hub for trade. After any declined/expired/withdrawn/failed AI offer, wait **5 completed boundaries** before another outgoing AI proposal to that counterpart. This cooldown does not block receiving/responding or a human proposal. Accepted offers have no cooldown. AI neither terminates nor bluffs in this initial conservative policy; termination is human control and shared command support for future AI. No mechanical difficulty or species personality chooser. Deterministic decision previews explain legal/threshold facts as of the current view; they are advisory until resolution and never disclose AI bank/hub/hidden maps.

## DIP-007 State, phases and compatibility

Plain serializable state comprises canonical unordered pair keys, reciprocal identity contacts, directed streak/cancellation dates, bounded pair-local counters/revisions, one pending offer with optional answer and private hub authorizations, up to two agreements with effective/warmup/minimum/notice dates, and AI cooldown. Store only current state and a bounded last-result summary per pair/kind; ordinary turn reports carry events. No unbounded negotiation transcript, dynamic executable policy, foreign raw-state callback, wall clock or new PRNG stream. Entity IDs remain their established types; a private hub reference is not public offer data.

Ordering within the existing atomic turn transaction: validate input and Ready; plan ordinary empire commands plus diplomacy against own pre-resolution knowledge; freeze economic/diplomatic snapshot; finalize offer outcomes/setup payments for future-effective agreements; apply current agreement termination/hub-loss boundaries; run ordinary economy with effects from agreements effective at the frozen snapshot; preserve existing movement/ameba/combat/founding phases with the frozen accord restrictions; advance, age streaks/memory/warmup and publish effective/ended agreements; refresh legitimate observations and reciprocal contact; project private reports; validate entire candidate before publication. Newly made contact cannot trigger negotiations until the next planning turn. Existing human automation consent and exact old phase ordering remain unchanged outside the new-rules branch.

Content defines setup500/output100/warmup3/minimum10/notice2/offer3/cooldown5/streakcap10/streakstep2/cancellation10/memory10 and thresholds0/10 in a validated diplomacy definition, with an explicitly recognized algorithm ID. Embed effective definitions in saves. Core values above are the reviewable defaults. Validate types, positive/bounded durations, arithmetic compatibility, known algorithms and references at content load with file/entry/field errors. Unknown/missing definitions fail new-rules creation even in Solo. Alternate content uses the same generic rules; no species-ID branches. Builder may bound configurable durations to 1..1000, amounts to 1..1000000 internal and score magnitudes to 0..1000, with checked long intermediates and clear incompatibility errors for impossible combinations.

Proposed compatibility: **rules11/writer12/67 explicit pairs**, adding schema12 support for rules1..11 to the existing56, including only12/11 for the new rules11. Do not accept invented old-schema/new-rules combinations. New games default to11 only after approved implementation. Rules1..10 retain their exact contacts (including one-sided contacts), planning, costs, RNG/global IDs, reports and future continuation; no silent migration or diplomacy fields/defaults on old rules. New writer can round-trip old rules with their established omissions. Before product changes, freeze genuine rules10 futures from the current product; retain every older frozen fixture and all existing saves/evidence.

Reject malformed pair order/duplicates/self-contact, unknown participants, asymmetric new-rules contact channels, invalid/stale/future timestamps, impossible streaks/counters, multiple pending/live kinds, invalid pending private owner references, earmarks exceeding bank, missing/duplicate earmarks and unsupported rules/field combinations. For an already activated trade agreement only, retain the historical hub ColonyId even if now missing or foreign; this is a valid pending-link-loss condition, not an input-validation rejection. The identifier must be positive and below the allocator, with an activation record binding it to that signatory; current ownership/existence is checked by the termination phase. Owner reports may name only their historical hub, and the partner gets the generic loss reason. Pending offers/earmarks still require current owned references and may never use this live-agreement exception. Do not require current foreign observation to validate a historically accepted accord. Validate structural/historical invariants without treating a current hidden world as a required public fact. Save/Load must retain offered/answered/earmarked, warmup, notice, cooldown and boundary semantics exactly. All dry-run queries are immutable and consume no IDs/RNG. Internal failed turn diagnostics must not quote foreign objects or intent.

## DIP-008 Player experience

Add Diplomacy to existing main navigation. Its list contains only contacted empires, both directed attitudes, pending-action badge and agreement summary; Solo/unmet states explain how contact works. Selecting a contact opens a conversation/detail panel with cultural introduction, exact attitude reasons, active agreements and dated terms. Offers use focused forms rather than a barter grid: accord system selector from own permitted observations; trade own-hub selector with earmark/payment/output/warmup forecast. Show a clear confirmation that sending discloses the named accord system, while trade hubs remain private. Show AI public policy reasons and “acceptance checked at resolution.”

Incoming offers remain visible until answered, withdrawn or expired, with remaining boundaries. Accept requires the complete terms and, for trade, an own hub; Decline is one action without punishment. Outgoing offers expose Withdraw, accepted answers expose Revoke before resolution, active agreements show the first legal termination turn, notice end and consequences. Existing founding UI explains accord blockage and links to the agreement. Existing colony reserve/queue/refit views expose earmarked/spendable production. Ready summary highlights incoming offers without forcing a response; no modal interrupts turn execution and no automatic treaty acceptance.

Use containers/anchors and the existing single theme; cultural text and statuses are original. Keyboard focus, accessible names, scrollable narrow-window content and text alongside colors are required. First contact, incoming offer, acceptance/decline, activation failure, termination notice and final ending are actionable reports; routine trade earnings and streak increments belong in breakdowns, not alert spam. Reopening/resizing/loading never replays a first-contact popup or changes state. Every view/report is requester-filtered, including diagnostics, exports and preview revisions. This document specifies behavior, leaving precise container layout to the builder; no visual mockup is needed to settle these mechanics.
