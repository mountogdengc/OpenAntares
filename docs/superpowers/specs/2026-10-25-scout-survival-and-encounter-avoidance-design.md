## 1. Intent, scope and the survival boundary

The owner wants scouts to try to survive/escape danger, with encounters offering alternatives to unavoidable tactical battle and loss. Speed, warning range and escape chance were suggested factors, not approved formulas. Broader fleet goals, preferred/forbidden targets and retreat/hold orders remain recorded intent.

Existing rivals remain peaceful expansion competitors. Foreign vessels and colonies are observable presence, **not an established threat**. The new danger is an original solitary space ameba inhabiting one system: it can consume vessels that linger, while timely departure gives a genuine survival opportunity. Its encounter resolves in the strategic simulation without a tactical battle. No armed rival, damage/repair framework, hostility relation, long-range sensor or transit interception is inferred.

Success is understandable standing orders and a meaningful risk choice: leave the ameba immediately and survive, or deliberately study it for a one-time research reward with stated chances of losing the scout. Avoiding known hazards costs routes and opportunities. Optional caution toward neutral empires remains separate from default survival against the ameba.

Includes two neutral-contact postures, own-view filtering, ordinary withdrawal, one ameba habitat, warning/survival defaults, bounded escape/study rolls, actual vessel/cargo loss, reports, AI and compatibility. Excludes automatic human outward exploration, roaming/reproduction/splitting, ameba attacks on colonies, monster combat/removal, fleet grouping, warships/design, escorts, hold-at-all-costs, tactical combat, capture, interstellar pursuit, mid-route diversion, long-range detection, new species traits, diplomacy, peaceful-mode design, victory, networking, new art and release. Cargo vessels obey the same hazard timing/loss, with manual departure and no study; ordinary disease/economy rules otherwise remain unchanged. The ameba is an original setting creature; no commercial game's name, appearance, text, art or exact behavior is a reference requirement.

## 2. SCT-001: Scout authority and two postures

Eligible roles are existing valid non-founding, zero-cargo vessel definitions, including core `scout`; use role capabilities, not a species or role-ID special case. Core cost8 production/speed300 and existing founding-role costs/speed200 remain unchanged. No research gate, fuel, maintenance resource, speed upgrade or free replacement is introduced.

- **Observe (default for neutral contact):** retain existing manual travel, arrival survey and peaceful rival co-location. It does not waive ameba survival orders or imply immunity to the hazard.
- **Avoid encounters (explicit opt-in per scout):** reject a destination with known foreign presence, and, when currently sharing a system with foreign presence, prepare an ordinary withdrawal if no permitted manual departure exists. This authorizes this scout's movement only. It never enables colony governors, crisis authority, research choices or automatic outward exploration.

All newly created human and AI scouts initially have Observe plus explicit **Withdraw from ameba** survival authority, visibly stated in New Game and the vessel inspector. Survival is a separate default standing order, not permission to avoid neutral empires or explore outward. Human neutral posture changes only through an ordinary empire-issued expected-turn command; new-rules AI selects Avoid through the same path. Survival is not globally disabled in this slice: a direct Study or Remain at risk order can override it for one named scout/ameba/boundary after displaying the consequences. No global takeover threshold, doctrine editor or batch command is required.

Neutral posture may change in transit, but cannot cancel, redirect or delay the committed route. Observe allows peaceful rival observation; it is not an ameba risk waiver. Ameba risk commands require a currently present owned stationary vessel, exact encounter revision and expected turn. They expire after one resolved boundary and cannot authorize later reinforcements or repeat studies. A known ameba destination requires explicit risk acceptance for an Observe scout's exact unstarted route; changing/clearing that route removes the acceptance. Avoid scouts reject it, and cargo vessels cannot be ordered toward a known ameba habitat. Unknown destinations remain legal for everyone without hidden rejection.

Turning on Avoid previews and atomically clears an unstarted move if its destination is excluded by the issuing empire's current view. Other permitted pending moves remain. Turning it off leaves any pending move intact, including an already prepared withdrawal; the player can clear/replace it through ordinary controls. Posture changes never erase surveys or contacts. A true no-op preserves the original state/reference/readiness; a changed posture or order clears only the issuer's Ready.

## 3. SCT-002: What a scout can avoid

A star is excluded for an empire when either of these facts appears in its permitted published view:

1. A currently or last-seen foreign colony is recorded on any surveyed planet there. A stale observation is still grounds for caution, explicitly labelled with its observation turn; it is not a claim that the colony still exists.
2. At least one stationary foreign vessel is currently visible there through ordinary friendly presence.

Additionally, a known ameba habitat is excluded under Avoid and from every automatic fallback regardless of neutral posture. Unlike transient foreign ships, the stationary permanent ameba is a saved static habitat observation per empire, learned only through current friendly presence. It survives loss of observation with a last-observed timestamp. It reveals no planet details before an ordinary arrival survey, foreign ameba sightings, study claims, streams or foreign losses. No global hazard count or location appears in an empire's campaign view before discovery.

No contact-only exclusion, culture-based hostility, unseen current occupancy, foreign destination/ETA, hidden colony count, foreign cargo/health, seed reconstruction or nearest-enemy query is permitted. Unknown stars remain legal outward travel targets. An empty stale observation means 'no foreign presence recorded', never 'safe'. Own colonies do not cancel a visible foreign presence in the same system.

Presence is system-wide for this voluntary scout policy only. It does not establish ownership of a star, impede another empire, close a port or stop an expedition. A foreign vessel sighting disappears when observation ends exactly as today. Do not retain its ID/role/location as an avoidance blacklist. Last-seen colony facts and contact identities persist by their existing rules. A new current empty observation replaces an old colony sighting normally.

Route previews distinguish neutral presence, known ameba habitat and unknown hazard status, with freshness and ordinary distance/speed/duration. They never claim unknown routes are safe. No extra sensor radius is introduced. Only the independent ameba streams in SCT-007 supply new draws; neutral avoidance and ordinary travel remain draw-free.

## 4. SCT-003: Withdrawal and manual orders

First apply ameba survival to every eligible scout, regardless of neutral posture: absent a direct current risk order, retain a permitted manual departure or prepare retreat to a known non-ameba star. Prefer own-colony systems, then surveyed systems; rank duration then StarId within the chosen group. Respect neutral Avoid exclusions if any such fallback exists; if none does, survival may select a surveyed neutral-occupied system using the same ranking and explicitly report that neutral avoidance yielded to survival. Never choose another known ameba habitat. If no permitted fallback exists, do not silently waive danger: retain the emergency/Ready blocker and require a permitted manual departure (an unknown star is allowed) or an explicit Remain at risk order. No-roll safe departure applies only in the warning window described in SCT-007.

Otherwise, for each stationary Avoid scout at the beginning of a planning preparation:

1. If it has a permitted manual pending departure, retain it. A normal human movement order to a non-excluded public star, including an unknown star, takes priority over automatic fallback.
2. If its current star is not excluded, do nothing. Avoid does not send a human scout outward, return it home merely because its survey finished, or move it because a contact exists somewhere else.
3. Otherwise choose a different retreat star with no known foreign presence. First consider own-colony systems; if none qualify, consider surveyed systems. Within the chosen group rank ordinary travel duration from the current star, then StarId. Surveyed fallback may be stale and must be labelled accordingly. Do not select an unvisited star automatically. Multiple scouts may use the same fallback.
4. If a candidate exists, issue the ordinary pending move through shared validation. A previously prepared departure to a now-excluded destination is cleared/replaced in this same preparation. If none exists, clear that excluded departure if present, remain stationary and publish persistent Attention: 'No known retreat destination; choose Observe or give a permitted destination.' There is no destruction, invented safe point, teleport, emergency speed boost or unbounded search.

An unstarted move that becomes excluded is handled at the next preparation even when the origin has no foreign presence: clear it; then withdraw only if the origin is also excluded. Ordinary selection of an excluded destination while Avoid returns a distinct own-knowledge rejection, without mutating an earlier order/readiness. Unknown/nonexistent or foreign vessel commands retain the existing generic validation/privacy behavior. Validate ownership before returning posture/presence-specific information.

Automatic decisions are computed when the human requests Ready, on the private candidate after the existing crisis/development preparation; views, forecasts, saves and non-Ready commands never prepare movement. A changed manual order can replace a prepared withdrawal before the empire becomes Ready again. A non-final human Ready may commit its own prepared move, as existing delegated planning does; final resolution failure returns the exact state before that final Ready request, preserving another human's earlier accepted orders.

Final-Ready preparation must not replan already-ready humans from other empires' later private choices. No observation refresh occurs during planning. A scout in transit never receives a move; a scout completed this resolution first becomes eligible in the next planning period. Arrival cannot cause a second departure during the same resolution.

## 5. SCT-004: Encounter timing, costs and reports

Retain the existing movement and observation boundary. Arrivals finish ordinary travel; final stationary presence reveals **all planets in that system**, ownership and visible stationary vessels, then establishes contacts normally. Avoid does not grant pre-arrival planet information, prevent a first contact already observed, or erase the survey because the scout intends to leave. If avoiding planetary discovery itself is desired, that is a separate change to the approved exploration model.

An unexpected foreign presence discovered at published turn T is an Attention fact for that scout. Earliest automatic departure begins in the next T-to-T+1 resolution after Ready; a duration2 withdrawal arrives at T+2. The scout supplies ordinary current observation while stationary at T. On departure its old system becomes last-seen unless another friendly vessel/colony remains. No intermediate transit position supplies observation, contact or interception. A foreign arrival after this scout departs does not create a transient same-system encounter.

Foreign vessels may depart simultaneously, leaving the scout to make an unnecessary withdrawal. Keep the order; do not inspect foreign plans or reverse a leg. Neutral encounters have no hull-loss roll, currency or population cost. Against the ameba, speed also affects the explicit late-escape/study odds in SCT-007; neither posture provides a hidden bonus. Ordinary production/speed values are unchanged.

Use an owner-only scout-order summary alongside ordinary arrival/contact reports. Show scout ID, posture, observed fact/freshness, fallback/manual departure, duration, cancellations and wait reasons. Neutral encounters stay Attention with no acknowledgement requirement. Ameba encounters have the separate saved warning/acknowledgement described below. Explain ordered/travelling/arrived and actual escape/study/loss outcomes accurately. Never describe neutral presence as an attack or expose foreign plans, cargo, home or health. Neither category satisfies a plague receipt.

Keep latest preparation/arrival outcome and a ten-turn owner-only history across save/load. Deduplicate a persistent wait by scout/current-star/reason until its condition changes; it remains visible at Attention even if routine summaries are Off. A deliberate posture change, selected destination, actual arrival or changed reason can generate a new record. Thirty waiting scouts use one grouped summary, no modal per ship. History records immutable own facts and permitted observations, not live references; an old foreign-vessel sighting retained in an explicitly historical order explanation never becomes a current map marker or planner input.

## 6. SCT-005: AI, bounded work and transaction order

Only proposed rules7 extends `expansion_v1`. Before vessel choice, AI selects Avoid for eligible Observe scouts through ordinary commands. Ameba survival takes precedence: scouts and cargo vessels depart promptly, never study or choose Remain at risk; known habitats are filtered out of exploration, fallback and founder candidates. Unknown habitats cannot affect planning. Keep the one-scout replacement target and actual construction/cargo costs, species economy, crisis prepass and stale knowledge. A loss causes ordinary paid replacement when existing production policy allows; never grant a free scout. No new personality or hidden threat scoring.

At vessel choice, first retain a permitted pending withdrawal/manual departure from an earlier accepted planning period; otherwise apply the withdrawal rule for current foreign presence. When not withdrawing/waiting, use the approved unknown-star or age-eligible resurvey ranking, filtering out excluded destinations before the existing own-scout destination deconfliction. If no eligible unknown target remains, try eligible resurvey candidates instead; if no candidates remain, wait. Under current knowledge rules, truly unknown stars carry no foreign presence fact and are therefore not excluded. Existing `resurvey_min_age` (core3) prevents immediate return to a now-stale vessel sighting's system; do not invent a permanent hidden-vessel memory. A scout may revisit once ordinary knowledge/age permits and then withdraw again. AI need not optimize around information it no longer has.

Rules7 expansion planning budget is at most2C+2V+1 accepted commands per AI empire: existing workforce/production/research allowance, plus at most one posture and one movement/founding/cancellation command per owned vessel. If an excluded pending move needs replacement, submit the replacement once rather than clear then set. Posture activation may itself cancel a pending move atomically. Existing separate crisis budget remains C+V+4. Rules4/5/6 retain their original budgets and algorithm behavior.

Human preparation emits at most one movement/cancellation command per eligible scout. Candidate enumeration is finite over its own view's stars/vessels/colonies, sorted stably; no repeated search until a route succeeds, population enumeration, background agent or wall-clock budget. Pure queries use the same preview/ranking rules as execution.

Keep the accepted economic/health/cargo/founding order. Proposed rules7 transaction:

1. Human Ready: existing crisis acknowledgement plus exact-current ameba warning acknowledgement, then private crisis/development/scout survival preparation; mark Ready only if the projected resulting departure/risk orders legally address every current ameba encounter. Purely previewing these orders does not mutate state. Direct Resolve validates the same ameba authorization and runs idempotent human preparation before AI planning; it cannot bypass acknowledgement/risk requirements.
2. AI empires in ascending EmpireId: existing crisis prepass, ordinary development/expansion choices plus scout posture/withdrawal/filtered exploration. Use the same pre-resolution observation boundary, refreshed only for own accepted planning changes.
3. Capture the planned snapshot. Run economy/completions and crisis progression. Resolve ameba departure/study/remain effects for pre-existing stationary encounters before crisis departure tagging/movement; remove consumed vessels/cargo, and ensure all downstream movement/founding/crisis/report phases consistently omit them. Survivors run existing cargo tagging, ordinary movement/arrival transport and founding; guarded habitats reject founding. Then existing exposures/onset/finalization. Empty scouts remain outside disease transport.
4. Advance once; refresh final observations/contacts and harmless new-arrival ameba encounters. Deposit earned study research after the ordinary economy/technology/cure phase; it is available from the next planning period and cannot cause a second completion/cure this boundary. Record warnings, reward/loss/escape facts and clear readiness; validate/publish once.

A command rejection unexpectedly emitted by preparation, bound violation, overflow, report/state invariant failure or late final validation error aborts the whole enclosing transaction. Preserve input reference/bytes, all readiness, both existing RNGs, IDs, observations, crises, governors, orders and reports. No partial evacuation, contact or cleared receipt. Public failure messages remain generic where AI/private facts are involved. A neutral 'no retreat' wait is a valid explained outcome; an ameba encounter with no legal departure/risk response blocks Ready as specified below. Preview/report reads consume no IDs or RNG draws.

## 8. SCT-007: Space ameba habitat, warning, escape and study

### Habitat and setup

Add visible **Space ameba: Standard (default) / Off** for new rules7 campaigns, independent of rival count and plague selection. This is a hazard setting, not implementation/design of FR-001 Peaceful mode. Standard creates at most one permanent stationary ameba. It does not roam, split, reproduce, chase between stars, attack colonies or get killed/cleared in this slice. No empire/species/contact record is created for it. It lives among the star's outer debris and consumes nearby vessels, leaving planets intact.

Generate the ordinary rules6 galaxy/homes/entities first. Candidate habitats are non-home stars with no colony, sorted by StarId. If fewer than two candidates remain, create none and explain the lack of eligible habitat in New Game; do not fail a valid small/custom campaign or expose which star would have been chosen. Otherwise use one bounded placement selection, leaving at least one uninhabited non-home system free of amebas. No planets, home placement, routes, economic types or original IDs change. A hazard-local ID1 is distinct from global entity allocation; NextEntityId is unchanged. In normal core maps, Standard therefore has exactly one ameba. Generation never consults species identity for habitat selection.

Use existing PCG32 with unsigned64-bit derivation: placement `FromSeed(galaxySeed XOR 0x414D454241000001, 0x4841424954415401)`, one `NextInt(candidateCount)` only when placement is eligible. Escape/study stream per empire: `FromSeed(galaxySeed XOR 0x414D454241000001, unsignedEmpireId XOR 0x4553434150450001)`. Save stream states/counters explicitly in Standard. Off has no hazard/encounters/claims and null streams, consumes no hazard draws, and never infers placement from missing metadata. Freeze literal seed/draw vectors in the separate plan. Existing galaxy/crisis/appearance streams are untouched.

Embedded JSON entry `space_ameba` has pursuit speed200; late escape base50%, relative-speed coefficient100, min5/max95; study base50%, coefficient50, min5/max90; reward600 stored research units (6 points). Validate positive speed/reward, bounded integer bases/coefficients and ordered0..100 probability bounds, using checked wide arithmetic. These balance values are modifiable through JSON. **Warning-departure success100% with no roll and one successful study reward per empire/habitat are algorithm invariants**, not modifiable fields; do not accept fields purporting to override them or silently ignore such overrides. No study technology, special scout build or species branch is added.

### Warning and default survival

On first arrival or later re-entry, complete ordinary travel/full survey/contact first, then publish a per-vessel ameba encounter with **Warning** and arrival turn T. Arrival causes no roll, damage or loss, irrespective of vessel speed or other empires' prior visits. All vessels arriving at that boundary get their own full response opportunity. No previous explorer's warning timer can make a new arrival die immediately. Hazard knowledge is private to each observing empire; the ameba itself is a permanent shared physical fact.

The warning guarantees a clean break **if the vessel departs in the next T-to-T+1 resolution**:100%, no draw, ordinary route/duration. It does not guarantee indefinite stationary safety. Before human Ready, display the creature, exact vessel/cargo at risk, safe-departure deadline, prepared route or lack of one, the lethal result of remaining, and optional study odds/reward. Require exact encounter-revision acknowledgement; acknowledgement only records informed review and cannot substitute for a valid departure or a direct risk order. All current encounters are addressed in one grouped overview with per-vessel orders and one exact-set acknowledgement. Another empire cannot acknowledge them.

Scouts automatically prepare withdrawal under default survival unless explicitly ordered to study/remain. Cargo vessels require an ordinary manual departure; no silent population movement authority is invented. Ready is blocked when a warning lacks acknowledgement or has no legal departure/risk order. An already committed withdrawal may arrive several turns later; success means breaking local contact, not instant return. Once transit begins there is no further ameba pursuit or draw.

Warning expiry is tied to one resolved response boundary, not clicks, wall time or save/load. If the vessel remains by explicit **Remain at risk**, the ameba consumes it at that boundary with certainty. Study is the only survivable stationary action; a successful study publishes **Exposed** at T+1. An Exposed vessel may attempt late escape next resolution, while a further deliberate Remain at risk consumes it. A fresh explicit risk order and current acknowledgement are required for each harmful stationary boundary; earlier consent never silently rolls over. Direct Resolve obeys the same requirements for humans. No disabled-display setting or unrelated crisis acknowledgement waives these safeguards.

### Late escape and study

For role speed s and pursuit speed p, with mathematical floor and checked wide integers:

- Late escape success percent = clamp(50 + floor(100 * (s-p) / p),5,95).
- Study survival percent = clamp(50 + floor(50 * (s-p) / p),5,90).

Use effective embedded JSON values in place of the literal bases/coefficients/caps. Core scout speed300 gives95% late escape and75% study survival; speed200 gives50%/50%. Prompt warning departure remains100% even for a slow cargo ship. Previews expose speed/pursuit/base/modifier/cap and distinguish study survival, later escape and combined eventual hull retention. Core study then late escape retains the hull with75%*95%=71.25%, displayed as an exact integer fraction or rounded display clearly labelled;75% is not advertised as guaranteed eventual return.

Only a currently stationary eligible scout can receive **Study ameba**. It replaces an unstarted departure after consequence preview and is a deliberate one-boundary override of default withdrawal; never repeat automatically. At most one pending Study per empire/habitat; reserve it through ordinary shared validation so a second scout cannot duplicate a reward. An empire that already earned the reward cannot study again. A failed study consumes the scout; a later paid replacement may try again until one succeeds. Other empires can earn their own single reward without sharing claims or intelligence.

Successful study leaves the scout Exposed and deposits600 research units into its empire's ordinary research reserve **after** this boundary's normal research/cure resolution. Do not change its target, divert an old reserve, complete a second technology, accelerate this turn's cure, or create a permanent technology. Next normal research uses the reserve under existing rules. Show the reward as a separate explained source in forecasts/history. Expected-turn orders/confirmation/previews consume no RNG; the actual study roll occurs once during resolution. A scout that survives may still be lost on next interval's late escape; earned research stays with the empire.

Process risk resolution by ascending EmpireId then VesselId. Prompt Warning departures use no draw. Exposed departure and Study each call that owner's `NextInt(100)` exactly once, success iff draw<displayed percent; use existing bounded-call rejection sampling. Deliberate Remain consumes with no draw. Commands, report reads, pure preparation, neutral encounters, arrival and invalid/blocked actions draw nothing. No cross-empire draw or free reroll on reload; canonical save/load futures must match. A normal safe destination order out of an Exposed encounter itself authorizes its displayed late-escape roll after acknowledgement; success starts ordinary movement, failure removes the vessel before movement.

### Loss, founding, report and AI integrity

Consumption removes exactly the vessel, its carried population, transit/pending/founding/risk orders and live crisis flags/holds. No population returns to its source, no reserve refund/free replacement, no colony loss or new plague exposure from destroyed cargo. Retain immutable own report facts/cargo loss, prior surveys/contacts and already-earned research. No floating damage/repair state is added. The hazard cannot occupy a colony star; founding in its habitat rejects using permitted current encounter/knowledge before ordinary eligibility, and no alternate founder-order path may bypass it. Route validation must never reveal an unknown ameba location through an error. Known-habitat founder destinations are unavailable; unsuspecting arrivals still receive the full warning/safe departure.

AI uses its own saved knowledge/ordinary commands and always chooses warning departure rather than study/remain; it receives no special survival chance or free cargo. Unknown ameba placement does not affect its targets. When its last colony is gone, a hazard survivor can still retreat to a surveyed unguarded system under the existing zero-colony policy; if no automatic fallback exists, a charted unknown non-current star is a lawful AI emergency destination, ranked duration/StarId. This blind escape is explicitly an AI survival choice, not hidden knowledge. Human scouts instead keep the blocker and offer that manual option. Cargo AI evacuation is part of its one existing movement operation, not a new founder expedition goal.

Loss creates an owner-visible critical outcome; an unresolved live encounter remains persistent Emergency and opens by default. Allow local Interrupt/Attention/Digest display levels while preserving a critical badge, exact Ready blocker and saved receipts; no invisible Off or automatic risk waiver. Order/history facts remain private; foreign observers see only the existing ordinary final stationary-vessel view, with no cargo/research/loss report or inferred cause of a vanished marker. Group scale cases without modal storms. Stored encounter revisions renew on Warning-to-Exposed change and relevant changed risk/consequence facts; receipts for departed/destroyed vessels retire without satisfying new arrivals. Rollback includes hazard streams, revisions, claims, research deposits, removed vessel/cargo facts and receipts.
