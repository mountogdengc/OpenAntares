## 2. FLT-001 — Central command and direct authority

Fleet Command is reachable without selecting a map object. It lists all own ships and fleets, their roles, current system or committed route, remaining turns, orders, admiral, build-request reservation and actionable exceptions. Filters include idle, travelling, newly completed/unassigned, assigned request, role and attention. Sorts are stable by selected visible field then ID. A ship newly completed stays marked until explicitly assigned/dismissed; opening a window does not acknowledge an emergency or alter simulation state.

Select individual ships or fleets and issue known-system destinations, Hold, merge/split where legal, assign/remove an admiral, or open the existing inspection/founding/ameba response controls. Destination selection is available in the window from the own chart; the map can be an optional locator. Movement targets systems; founding still explicitly selects an eligible planet and submits the existing separate command. Present planet names with their parent system so the UI does not suggest movement itself founds a colony.

Bulk actions preview every selected target, effect, estimate and rejection. Apply is atomic across the selection: any stale, foreign, unavailable or illegal member rejects all, with useful owner-visible reasons. No silent subset. Offer an explicit revised selection rather than silently dropping failures. Identical valid orders are no-ops; real planning changes clear only the issuer's Ready. Window reads and save/load are pure.

Manual control needs no officer or technology. A manual ship order first requires explicit detachment from an incompatible fleet/request, with a before/after preview. Detachment pauses that request's affected slot; it does not silently order a replacement. Direct manual project changes pause the request's colony contribution without erasing unrelated queue entries or the colony's stored governor plan. Officers never silently reattach a detached ship or undo a manual order.

## 3. FLT-002 — Fleets, movement and standing orders

A fleet is an own named group of 1..32 vessels, at most64 active named fleets per empire, with a stable local ID and 1..80-character name. A vessel belongs to at most one fleet; independent ships remain fully controllable from Fleet Command. Ordinary completion creates an independent ship, not a new counted fleet. Pending reinforcements are reservations, not remote fleet members. Creating/merging/splitting named fleets requires stationary co-location, matching ownership and no active ameba encounter or cargo-transport hold. Fleet selection does not bypass per-vessel legality. At the64-fleet cap, discretionary creation rejects; construction and emergency withdrawal never fail merely because that cap is reached. Request acceptance reserves its one future named rally-fleet slot unless it targets an existing stationary rally fleet; cancellation releases that reservation. Count active plus reserved slots against64. A request with no reserved slot rejects before construction authority is granted.

Choose a flagship in each fleet. Assignment defaults to the lowest VesselId and is explicitly displayed. An admiral occupies that flagship for command mechanics; transfer requires co-located stationary fleets and is an atomic direct command. No instant cross-galaxy officer reassignment. An unassigned/recovered officer can be posted at an owned colony system and boards a stationary fleet there. Moving an officer to another system requires travel aboard a vessel under ordinary rules; no separate free officer transport unit.

Group movement commits all members to one origin/destination and one duration based on the slowest effective travel speed. Existing integer travel calculation remains: smallest positive n with (n*speed)^2 >= squared distance. The speed and duration are frozen at launch. No mid-transit redirection, merge, split, bonus change or loss-driven acceleration. Mixed scout300/settler200 over distance301 takes2 turns. Identical-route fleets do not merge automatically. Foreign observations remain the ordinary per-vessel view; no foreign fleet identity/admiral/mission disclosure.

Standing orders are Hold, Assemble at a selected system, Explore, and Return to a selected own-colony system. They are explicit, pausable and removable, and show the next proposed action/reason. No arbitrary scripts or order graph. Assemble/Return complete on arrival and become Hold. If a Return colony disappears or becomes unavailable, stop with Attention at the next legal stationary boundary; never invent another home or redirect transit. A newly completed ship can be reserved/attached after production but cannot depart until the next planning boundary.

Explore is scout-only and chooses one destination for its fleet at each stationary planning boundary. Prefer own-chart unsurveyed stars; if none, surveyed stars last observed at least5 turns ago. Exclude known ameba habitats and destinations forbidden by any member's current neutral posture; unknown presence remains unknown. Rank by travel duration at fleet speed, then StarId. After a successful arrival/survey, plan again on the next boundary. No candidate means Hold with a deduplicated completion/blocked reason, never a repeated resurvey loop or free ship. Explore never chooses Study/Remain, changes posture or acknowledges emergencies. Default fleet order is Hold; existing neutral Observe and ameba withdrawal remain independent.

Safety takes priority over standing orders. Active encounter responses still use each vessel's actual role, speed, cargo, revision and consent. Ordinary withdrawal may separate a fleet into departing and remaining ships; retain the flagship's named fleet/cohort and detach incompatible other members as independent ships in ascending VesselId order. Detached safety departures keep their actual individual route/duration and do not allocate extra counted fleets, so safety cannot exceed the named-fleet cap. The UI may group matching departures for display without creating authoritative fleets. If the flagship is lost, keep the surviving co-located compatible cohort under the existing ID with the lowest surviving VesselId as new flagship; other survivors become independent. If none remain, retire the named fleet. This safety split is resolver/preparation behavior only, not permission for arbitrary manual splitting during a live encounter. A manual Study command applies only to the chosen scout and separates it from an incompatible moving group; its explicit preview includes separation and officer consequences. No fleet-wide implicit risk waiver.

Successful colony founding consumes the colony vessel through ordinary founding, not destruction. If it carried an officer, post that officer at the newly founded own colony with unchanged XP; no recovery roll, death, recovery delay or loss counter. For Wenth, post the command organ while preserving the original source-body identity; founding does not turn it into the daughter body's officer. The remaining fleet Holds with the lowest surviving member as flagship, or retires if empty. A fleet/request reservation on a founding vessel cannot be silently fulfilled by consuming it: explicit manual founding detaches that request slot before execution and reports it Missing.

## 4. FLT-003 — Finite construction and assembly requests

A request specifies a name, known rally system, a finite composition by existing permitted vessel role, an explicit participating-colony set, and Normal/Urgent priority (Normal initially). The owner retained this priority control during fleet/construction review on2026-10-27. Limits:32 active requests/empire;32 total ship slots/request;32 participating colonies. Repeated roles are legal. No unknown roles, foreign colonies, automatic colony recruitment or unlimited replacement. Each colony participates in at most one active request; each vessel satisfies at most one slot. A rally fleet serves at most one active request and reserves capacity for its full proposed composition, with existing members counted once and total membership capped32. A conflicting manual merge, departure or member reassignment requires explicit pause/detachment of the request first. Disbanding its rally fleet pauses the request until an explicit replacement rally fleet/slot is approved; no capacity or paid construction is silently discarded. Request authorization is separate from a governor template.

The preview offers eligible idle ships first (only stationary, unreserved, outside encounters/holds, with no pending manual move/founding and no admiral assigned elsewhere), ranked arrival duration then VesselId. Their use requires explicit acceptance. Remaining slots are assigned to participating colonies by earliest estimated completion-plus-arrival, then ColonyId, then slot number. Recalculate the colony's estimated tail after each assigned slot. Estimate each candidate using its own current workforce/output and hypothetical queue after the selected Normal/Urgent insertion, including its active project and prior assigned slots; label estimates conditional on unchanged workforce, technology, growth, quarantine and player orders. Unknown/infinite estimates cannot outrank finite estimates; unallocatable slots remain explicitly blocked and are not silently waived. No hidden future knowledge or guaranteed completion claim.

Accepting a Normal request appends finite, uniquely bound vessel entries after all existing explicit queued work. Urgent inserts its entries before unstarted explicit work, preserving the active project and its binding ahead of them. Both preserve production reserve and the relative order of unrelated entries. Existing governor template suggestions remain behind authorized explicit queued work. Urgent changes queue priority only; it does not grant extra production, bypass prerequisites/embarkation/quarantine, or cancel/replace an active project. An active but blocked project still waits; the preview explains that Urgent cannot bypass it. Replacing that project requires a separate explicit manual replacement. Where ordinary construction automation is off, it grants a separate temporary request authority to execute the finite explicit queue through its last request-bound entry; it does not toggle construction/workforce switches or grant authority to use the stored template. The preview shows prior and proposed queue order, active-project retention, which explicit entries will run first, and changed conditional completion/arrival estimates. The shared selection path chooses the first legal explicit entry only; if none is legal it banks output and reports, never falls through to template construction. If ordinary construction automation was already on, its existing queue/template authority remains and only the existing governor selector chooses the active project. Thus there is one selector per colony, never two competing controllers. Once the last request-bound entry completes or contribution is paused/cancelled, temporary selection authority ends immediately; retained ordinary switches/template are unchanged. An active ordinary project may finish afterward, matching existing semantics. If an unqueued manual project is active, it finishes first. Existing32-entry per-colony queue bounds still apply: a plan that would overflow rejects atomically. There is no exclusive shipyard building or new production pool.

Changing priority later previews an atomic reorder of only that request's unstarted entries: Normal-to-Urgent moves them after the preserved active project and before unrelated pending work; Urgent-to-Normal moves them to the tail. Keep request-entry relative order, all queue tokens/bindings, reserve and unrelated-entry order. A same-priority no-op leaves readiness unchanged; a real accepted edit clears the issuer's Ready. Editing a paused request does not resume selection/dispatch. Cancellation with Keep queued construction retains the accepted queue positions while converting entries to ordinary finite orders; Remove unstarted entries removes only that request's unstarted entries. No reorder occurs during reads, reloads or officer promotion.

Population-carrying roles need a second explicit cargo authorization in the request preview: per-ship embarkation, aggregate population taken from each colony, species maturity/habitat constraints and resulting forecasts. If absent, reject those role slots. Existing ordinary completion/snapshot, quarantine and founding rules apply. A request never creates population, transfers colony workforces remotely or auto-founds. Cargo blocked at completion waits and reports; no automatic substitution with another vessel role.

Completion identifies the exact reserved queue token, role, colony and request slot. It marks that slot Built, reserves the new independent vessel and gives it an Assemble order for the next boundary. Surplus hulls, earlier manually built matching ships and ships built after cancellation do not secretly count. Arriving ships join a stationary rally fleet only when legal; otherwise wait separately with Attention. The reserved named-fleet slot permits creating that rally fleet at the first arrival without competing with later discretionary creation. A request becomes Complete only when every currently authorized slot is Arrived and its full composition quota is fulfilled; any Missing, blocked, paused, queued or travelling slot prevents completion. A reduced quota needs an explicitly accepted amendment. Completion leaves the assembled fleet Holding and issues one report.

Pausing stops future request planning/dispatch and pauses contribution selection while preserving the active project, finite queue bindings, built ships and manual orders. The already-active ordinary vessel project may still finish; its ship waits. Resume previews the retained work. Cancel offers two explicit choices: Keep authorized queued construction, converting its request-bound entries to ordinary finite queue entries; or Remove unstarted request entries, retaining active projects, all unrelated entries and reserve. Either cancels dispatch/reservations and never scraps ships/refunds spent output. A manually detached/lost assigned ship leaves a visible Missing slot; filling it needs an explicit finite amendment/approval. Retained already-launched travel still arrives normally.

Updating desired composition is a new reviewed amendment, not hidden live propagation. Explain request reservations, queued/built/travelling/arrived/missing totals and any double-use conflict. Manual takeover pauses only the affected contribution/slot. A governor and admiral cannot independently replace the same active project: only one shared planning path selects it, using authorized finite entries and existing governor precedence.

## 5. FLT-004 — Named officers, appointments and species identity

For Peldra/Harl/Sulan, each new human empire starts with one named unassigned Navigator at its home system and two reserve candidates, a Surveyor and Organizer. Each candidate has authored name/pronouns, species/culture reference, personality text and a fixed specialty. Choose a candidate from Fleet Command; commissioning takes2 completed turns, then they are available at the selected owned colony. No currency or population recruitment fee is introduced. At most4 simultaneously commissioned/commissioning officers and64 lifetime commissioned officers; candidates are offered from an embedded finite name catalog, using deterministic suffixes after exhaustion. Dismissal is permanent, preserves service history and never refunds XP; retiring/dismissing/recruiting cannot clone a career.

The Wenth exception is explicit: a named admiral is the whole Wenth body exercising a command appointment through a remote organ/link, not a new person, speaking-limb leader or removable workforce lobe. Each owned Wenth colony can supply at most one active appointment, within the same4-officer cap. The home appointment starts as Navigator; later appointments choose a specialty at commissioning. The initial home body supplies the first appointment; later appointments require another own colony, not fictional independent candidates. Names identify the body, with an appointment suffix for UI uniqueness. They pay no population and do not create a second body. Flagship/link loss never kills their source colony. Recovery and service consequences differ as defined in FLT-006. These are proposed command abstractions for review, not amendments to Wenth biology.

Every officer record stores stable own ID, profile/culture at commissioning, specialty, XP/rank/unlocks, location/flagship/appointment status and bounded service history. Display personality in advice/reports; it does not grant hidden permission, alter orders randomly or produce unannounced modifiers. Names and fixed profiles come from original embedded data. Initial candidate/profile ordering is content order, not wall time or gameplay RNG; no market, bids, hiring rerolls, aging, natural death, loyalty/revolt, officer diplomacy or combat statistics in M8.

It never changes vessel ameba survival behavior, permits Study/Remain, overrides a player's departure or alters cargo loss. Current M8 has no combat/surrender/capture/rescue mission. A human fleet may attempt a risky study only through the existing explicit scout command and consent.
