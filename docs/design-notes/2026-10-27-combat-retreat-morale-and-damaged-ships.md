# Future combat direction: retreat, morale and damaged ships

2026-10-27. **Status: Recorded owner direction for future combat design; not an approved specification, milestone or implementation task.** Current M8 remains fleet command/construction and named admirals using existing peaceful rivals and stationary ameba. This note does not add damage, morale or combat rules to M8 or change approved scout survival/cargo loss.

## Owner direction

- Future combat should consider retreat and morale rules.
- Ships could lose capabilities before being destroyed, enabling damaged vessels to limp home and making withdrawal meaningful.
- A slow damaged ship might constrain a fleet; choosing whether to keep the group together or leave/detach it could create a consequential decision.
- Hit-and-run tactics and disengagement should be possible design outcomes.
- The owner favors avoiding added mandatory per-ship micromanagement. Their preferred direction is substantial automation, potentially with a manual option; exact control depth remains open.
- The owner referenced a perceived all-or-nothing effectiveness pattern in MoO2 as motivation. Its precise commercial rules have not been independently verified here; this records the desired OpenAntares experience rather than adopting a factual claim or copying formulas.

## Questions for that future bounded design

What capabilities degrade, whether damage is discrete states or a numeric model, and how damage affects speed/weapons/other systems remain undecided. Morale could apply to crew, ship, fleet or command, but no resource, species modifier, threshold or probability has been selected.

Define retreat opportunities, destinations, timing, pursuit/interception, repair, disabled-ship fate and what commanders can authorize before implementation. Distinguish ordinary withdrawal, detaching a slow ship to return separately, abandoning it, and destroying/scuttling it; those actions have different consequences and are not interchangeable permissions.

Potential doctrine examples for exploration during design are keep the fleet together, prioritize fleet escape, detach impaired ships for an independent return, or hold despite losses. These are drafter prompts, not approved orders, defaults or automated sacrifice authority. Explain which ships detach, why, their likely outcome and the fleet's resulting movement. Use permitted knowledge and ordinary deterministic rules.

Culture/personality may suggest a doctrine if separately approved, while the player retains clear standing-order limits and manual override. Automatic behavior should reduce repetitive management and report meaningful exceptions, rather than require a tactical order for every damaged ship. Preserve the owner's uncertainty about exact manual control; do not silently choose fully hands-off or mandatory tactical control.

## Relationship to current rules

The proposed M8 slowest-member fleet travel can provide a foundation, but M8 currently has no damaged-speed or combat state. Named admirals, service records and editable doctrine are useful foundations, not authorization for warfare, casualty-triggered morale, repairs, sensors or pursuit. Existing harmless ameba Warning, explicit Study/Remain, hull/cargo loss and50/70% officer recovery retain their own contracts.
