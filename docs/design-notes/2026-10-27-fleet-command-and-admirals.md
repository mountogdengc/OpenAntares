# Owner direction: fleet command and named admirals

2026-10-27. **Recorded intent; proposed gameplay details remain unapproved.**

- One fleet command window should find/direct ships without searching the galaxy and selecting each separately.
- Finding completed ships across shipbuilding colonies and placing construction orders across colonies are related pain points, similar to the problems governors address.
- Admirals are an invited direction, open to refinements; named characters could be fun.
- Bonuses, a service record, simple experience and ability unlocks are desired.
- Officer-loss responses might start from species-culture defaults, be manually adjustable and possibly involve research. Exact policies/outcomes and research relationship are unsettled.
- Supplied Grok notes favor guided templates/policies, visible reasons, bulk application and manual override. Their references to commercial games/patches are unverified prompts, not project requirements or historical evidence.

The [draft](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-fleet-command-and-admirals-design.md) proposes a bounded first slice using current scouts/colony vessels: central commands, finite multi-colony assembly requests, explicit officer authority and small passive progression. Role/design-aware fleets, automatic retrofits, replenishment, combat-dependent patrol/escort and broader research/diplomacy automation remain a later design backlog. No owner examples or drafter formulas are silently treated as full approval. Preserve the uncommitted species roster/research/peaceful backlog and the Wenth whole-body/speaking-limb distinction.
