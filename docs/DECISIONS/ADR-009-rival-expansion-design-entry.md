# ADR-009: Select rival expansion for the next design

Date:2026-10-24. **Status: Accepted direction and drafting authority only. Full gameplay design approval pending.**

After the species milestone was integrated, the owner asked to continue. The [readiness assessment](../../reports/NEXT_MILESTONE_READINESS_2026-10-24.md) recommended rival expansion. In response to “Is competition with another empire the experience you want next?”, the owner replied “yes”. This selects that next player experience and authorizes the [rival-expansion specification](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-rival-expansion-design.md) for review.

It does not approve a particular opponent count, AI policy, contact model, version transition, implementation plan or code. Those are explicit proposals in the full design. Preserve classic space-opera direction, all approved species/expansion mechanics, original owner prose edits and genuine old campaigns. The existing Native execution preference remains available after the later design/plan gates; do not ask the owner to choose the same method again.

Full-design review precedes separate implementation planning. No rival gameplay/core-content/dependency/art changes, migration, push or cleanup are authorized by this design-entry decision. Human comprehension remains Deferred under ADR-005; it does not block this design work.
