# ADR-007: Species draft and creative-review authority

Date: 2026-10-23. **Status: Accepted authorization to draft; gameplay proposals remain unapproved.**

## Context and owner direction

After expansion integration, the owner requested a species-mechanics specification using the roster and a return for review before implementation. The owner then clarified that the existing creative decisions may be changed if needed. The later instruction supersedes the earlier request to preserve all creative decisions as fixed constraints. The owner subsequently clarified the accepted tone: a love letter to Master of Orion II, with fanciful humanoid animals/monsters and classic space-opera archetypes welcome. Scientific plausibility and contemporary representational checklists are not requirements; real peoples, cultures, religions and ethnic groups must be treated respectfully.

## Decision and boundary

Draft numeric economy, setup, habitats, production/founding and save compatibility; propose creative amendments where they improve coherence or play. Separate those author recommendations from owner-approved choices. Preserve the existing uncommitted roster/appearance and research edits rather than rewriting them as accepted amendments.

The [candidate specification](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-species-mechanics-design.md) is a review draft. Its four retained identities, population labels, viable openings, Harl recovery, Sulan harbor kit, Wenth maturity, numbers, Harl default and schema-4/rules-3 policy are not approved by this ADR. No species code, core-content edits or implementation plan is authorized by this record. Full owner review comes first; a separate implementation plan follows approval.

## Consequences

REQ-016 becomes In Progress for specification review only. REQ-017 remains Deferred. Existing rules-1/2 contracts, integrated expansion and legacy campaign behavior are unchanged. The unavailable uncoached session remains Deferred; its absence is not converted into human acceptance.
