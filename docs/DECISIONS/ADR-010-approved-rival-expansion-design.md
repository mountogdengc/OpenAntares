## Accepted scope

One rival by default, with visible Solo retained; existing four species for both sides, Harl/Peldra defaults and mirrors. Equal species-defined starts and ordinary costs, support, growth, cargo, habitats and snapshot rules. One deterministic expansion_v1 policy explores, develops, researches and settles through ordinary commands using only permitted information and scoped shared forecasts. Planning and turn resolution form one atomic transaction. Contacts and sightings disclose observed, dated facts. Existing EmpireId/VesselId founding priority, including the human's exact-tie advantage, is retained.

The approved implementation target is rules 4/writer 5 and the eleven explicit supported pairs; old rules 1/2/3, including passive Ai tags, retain genuine continuation behavior. No diplomacy, warfare, victory, difficulty bonuses, new species/art/dependencies or broad later systems are included.
