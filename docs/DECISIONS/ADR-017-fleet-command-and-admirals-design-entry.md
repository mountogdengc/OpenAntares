# ADR-017: Fleet Command and named-admiral design entry

Date: 2026-10-27. **Status: Recorded owner direction and drafting authority; full proposed design not approved.**

After local scout/ameba integration, the owner described a command window that finds/directs all ships and manages construction without visiting every world. They invited refinements and automation suggestions, suggested assigning admirals, chose named characters with bonuses, and requested a service record, simple experience and ability unlocks. They suggested culture-based officer-loss defaults with manual adjustment, possibly research-gated. Their supplied Grok notes advocate guided, visible, overridable policies and preserve wider ship-design/logistics/research/diplomacy ideas as future prompts; their external historical claims have not been independently verified.

This authorizes the [bounded M8 draft](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-fleet-command-and-admirals-design.md), reconciliation and one fresh read-only builder check. Fleet speeds/caps, finite allocation/authority, officer caps/commissioning, three passive tracks/XP, exact cultural defaults, Wenth embodiment, recovery probabilities/timers, research policy and rules8/writer9 are drafter proposals for full review. The owner has not approved those formulas by selecting the general direction.

M8-D/FLT-AC-012 In Progress; M8-P/M8-I and functional FLT acceptance Not Started. Current main remains c554797/rules7/writer8/29 pairs. M7/M6 acceptance, REQ-017 and its17-row totals, human/platform/security/export/release limits remain unchanged. Preserve all four primary owner edits, all seven existing worktrees/branches, QA/recovery and genuine old campaigns. No product code/content/default/schema change, implementation plan/execution, main integration, push/release or cleanup is authorized here. Full gameplay review precedes a separate implementation plan; retain Native after those gates, with no repeated method choice.


## Owner-confirmed doctrine/research decision — 2026-10-27

The owner selected: “Let’s keep doctrine editable from the start and have research improve recovery equipment.” Doctrine changes therefore need no technology unlock; research improves equipment. This settles only that policy, superseding the earlier open research-gate alternative. It does not approve the full M8 design, cultural defaults, recovery probabilities/delays, ability/XP values, technology cost/prerequisites, AI or rules8/writer9. M8-D/FLT-AC-012 remain In Progress; plan/implementation remain Not Started. No code or primary-main change.

## Fleet/construction review and request priority — 2026-10-27

After the fleet/construction walkthrough, the owner retained the proposed direction including Normal/Urgent priority. Normal is the initial priority and preserves existing explicit queue order. Urgent previews moving requested ships before unstarted work while preserving the active project; replacement remains a separate direct choice. The draft spells out priority-aware estimates, atomic later reordering, stable tokens/reserve, pause/cancel behavior and persisted priority. It retains coherent slowest-member travel, finite assembly, automatic rally dispatch, manual authority and explicit missing-slot amendments from the walkthrough. No new damage/morale/combat behavior is included. Remaining commissioning/Wenth/recovery-technology and other unconfirmed details/full M8 approval remain pending; plan/implementation Not Started.
