# ADR-005: Defer the uncoached playtest and proceed to expansion design

- **Status:** Accepted owner instruction, 2026-10-22.
- **Decision source:** In this conversation, after being offered deferral of the human acceptance step to proceed with exploration/colonization design, the owner said: "Let's defer it. I don't have anyone handy for that aside from myself".
- **Requirements:** REQ-014, REQ-015; AC-014, AC-015.
- **Supersedes:** The requirement in [ADR-004](ADR-004-spiral-galaxy-and-scope-gates.md) and the original validation plan that First Playable closure must precede exploration/colonization **design**, for the specific progression decision below.

## Context

The local technical baseline passes 195 simulation tests and 463 graphical runtime assertions. An uncoached participant unfamiliar with source is unavailable. Previous owner-reported functional passes remain valid historical evidence; the owner is not automatically counted as an unfamiliar participant. Complete physical-input/readability and player-understanding acceptance remain unverified to their stated limits.

## Decision

Defer the uncoached First Playable playtest, retaining the observation procedure and its requirement. Proceed now with exploration, travel and second-colony **design and review** using the existing architecture and validated economy. Do not repeatedly block this authorized design work on finding another participant or rerunning technical tests.

This is a documented exception to the design-entry gate. It does not turn missing observations into a pass, mark First Playable fully Verified, waive known defects, close release acceptance, or authorize broad feature implementation. A reviewed design and separate implementation plan remain necessary before gameplay coding.

## Consequences and revisit

REQ-014 becomes Deferred; REQ-015 design work becomes In Progress. The First Playable record remains open with this explicit deferral, while M2-D becomes the active milestone. Existing physical-input/readability limitations remain recorded and do not become inferred failures or new requirements to repeat checks now.

The risk is that later usability/balance observations could require revisions to the expansion design. Retain the human session for a future suitable participant or a later explicit owner decision; ordinary owner feedback is still useful. No deadline, participant recruitment, schedule, or new recurring task is created.
